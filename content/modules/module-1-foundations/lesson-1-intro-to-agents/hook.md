# Your flight to Berlin is cancelled — what happens next?

Imagine an AI travel concierge running on your phone. It observes that your flight to Berlin has been cancelled. 

Instead of waiting for you to panic and search flight boards, it reasons that you must still reach Berlin tonight, checks alternate flights and trains, books the optimal combination, and immediately messages your hotel regarding your delayed arrival.

How does a system move from simply answering questions to independently solving problems across the real world?
