In artificial intelligence, an **AI agent** is a system that can perceive its environment, make decisions, and act autonomously to achieve specific goals. This continuous loop—commonly summarized as **'perceive-reason-act'**—separates active, goal-seeking agentic systems from passive, query-response AI models.

> **In Plain English:** If an AI model is like a calculator waiting for you to type an equation, an AI agent is like a personal assistant who listens, figures out what needs doing, and takes real actions on your behalf.
