# Lesson 5 Recap: Key Takeaways

- **Layered Defense:** Guardrails protect every junction of the agent loop: **Input filtering** (prompt injections), **Tool safeguards** (allowlists & rate limits), and **Output validation** (schemas & safety).
- **Responsible Memory Retention:** Scrub PII before saving to memory, configure TTL expiration policies, and restrict recall of sensitive topics.
- **Spectrum of Human Oversight:** Tailor oversight to consequence: use **HITL** for irreversible/high-stakes steps, **HOTL** for real-time monitoring and pause controls, and **HATL** for audit logs and continuous improvement.
