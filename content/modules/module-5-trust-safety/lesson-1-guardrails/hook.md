# Your agent just transferred $10,000 to the wrong account. How do we prevent this?

It's one thing for a simple chatbot to generate a typo or hallucinate a recipe.

It's an entirely different disaster when an autonomous agent cancels an enterprise subscription, leaks confidential patient health records to a public forum, or triggers automated production code deployment without checks.

As agents gain real-world autonomy, trust becomes our highest design priority. How do we build systems that are safe, predictable, and firmly accountable?
