# Your agent can think and act — but what happens when the task requires an entire specialized team?

Imagine tasking an AI system with: *"Analyze competitor product updates, audit customer reviews for sentiment pain points, query internal sales revenue from SQL, synthesize findings, and produce a board-level slide deck."*

If you stuff all of this into a single prompt, the model hallucinates, drops steps, and exceeds context limits. 

How do we coordinate multiple specialized agents working sequentially, in parallel, and through clear handoffs without chaos?
