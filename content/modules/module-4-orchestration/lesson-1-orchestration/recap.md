# Lesson 4 Recap: Key Takeaways

- **Single-Agent Patterns:** Pick **Tool Calling Loops** for flexible exploration, **ReAct** for step-by-step transparency and auditable thought traces, or **Plan-and-Execute** for structured sequential milestones.
- **Multi-Agent Coordination:** Scale beyond single-model context limits using **Manager-Worker** (hierarchical delegation and synthesis) or **Decentralized Handoffs** (dynamic peer-to-peer control flow).
- **Framework Grounding:** Frameworks like **LangGraph** (graph-based execution), **CrewAI** (role-based crews), and **AutoGen** (dialogue swarms) provide standard abstractions for state, tools, and human oversight.
