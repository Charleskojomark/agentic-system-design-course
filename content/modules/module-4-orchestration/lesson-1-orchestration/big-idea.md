Orchestration defines how an agent manages its internal decision-making flow and, in multi-agent systems, how autonomous agents coordinate across shared goals. Orchestration patterns range from single-agent workflows (Tool Calling Loops, ReAct, Plan-and-Execute) to multi-agent architectures (Manager-Worker hierarchies and Decentralized Handoffs).

> **In Plain English:** If an agent is a musician, orchestration is the sheet music or conductor that tells each musician when to play solo, when to follow a script, and when to pass the melody to a partner.
